Privacy

Praxia Health. Last updated 9 October 2026.

Praxia Health collects no data. Everything the app records stays on the device it was recorded on.

In plain words

  • The app makes no network connections. It does not need the internet and never uses it.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads.
  • There are no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, notifications or Bluetooth.

What is kept on the device

To work as a game app, Praxia Health keeps a few things in its own private storage on the device:

  • Play history: which game, when, the score and how long it lasted.
  • Settings: language, text size, colours, contrast and similar choices.
  • Profiles: the names and colours a caregiver chooses, with each profile's own history.
  • Optional items a caregiver adds: a short note shown on the home screen, recommended games, and a 4-digit PIN. The PIN is stored scrambled (salted and hashed), never as typed.

None of this is sent anywhere. Removing the app from the device removes all of it.

Exports

A caregiver can choose to export the play history and the activity log as files. An export is created only when the caregiver chooses it, and it is shared only through the iOS share sheet, to the place the caregiver picks (for example Files, Mail or AirDrop). The app itself never sends it.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so and the app's store listing will be updated before a new version is released.
